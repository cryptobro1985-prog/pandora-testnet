# Feature and collector audit

The unchanged canonical contract has 116 points: 86 inputs, 28 target/evaluation fields and two metadata fields. Target/evaluation aliases and unsupported names are rejected before inference; current per-point availability is additive to the legacy ever-seen counter. Ever-seen86 does not mean86 fresh causal inputs now.

Implemented gates distinguish exchange source, receipt and inference clocks. Future, stale, unknown, missing and incomplete observations are not fabricated as observed zero. Ticker deltas retain per-field clocks; a fresh unrelated delta cannot refresh old OI/mark/funding values. Closed candle features require both finalization and actual receipt. The REST collector records completion time after requests, not the start of a potentially long poll. A new orderbook needs a snapshot; invalidated sequences stop book evidence, snapshot depth is not cancel/add churn. Raw unreceived or future-source frames cannot enter the causal feature bus.

Exact public-trade high/low price and dual clocks are exposed between prediction ticks. The runtime does not substitute a sampled book mid for an H1 traded extreme. Proxy-only proposals are watch-only and cannot create false negative supervised examples. This parity is tested with a transient trade spike between micro snapshots.

Historical findings and hashes are in FEATURE_AVAILABILITY.json and DATA_INTEGRITY.json. There are6261 snapshots,1797 candidates,43198 minute bars with two genuine missing minutes,77 strict and123 internal teachers. Historical source/receipt latency, L50 and liquidation coverage are not recovered by invention. Mark/index/OI/funding joins are audited and conservative, but their old receipt times remain unknown. Original five-second/minute snapshot inputs are contaminated and discarded for new fitting. Only completed-minute summaries and past stage changes are fitted; the richer causal live bus supports collection and later independent evaluation.

Tests cover source/receipt causality, per-field freshness, missing streams, partial candles, target aliases, book reset/churn and REST completion clocks. Legacy full-feature smoke assertions were retained and their synthetic inputs refreshed with newly received completed candles; staleness limits were not weakened. Final software evidence is in validation/current/REPORT.json. No monitor/relay routes or UI were changed.
