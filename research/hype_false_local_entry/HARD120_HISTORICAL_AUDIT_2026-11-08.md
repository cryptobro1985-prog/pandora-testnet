# HYPE INTERNAL HARD120 — historical research audit

This is a separate paper-only hard-confirmation layer for broad INTERNAL locals. It does not replace the early learner and it cannot flip the core direction.

Rule: the candidate must originate as a 6h trailing extreme, survive two hours without a more-extreme same-side 6h candidate, retrace at least 40 bps, and recover at least 45% of the causal 180-minute move into the extreme.

On the 120 fully actionable INTERNAL swings in the 2026-10-04..2026-11-03 archive, the first actionable signal was correct 52 times and false 5 times: **91.23% precision, 43.33% recall**. LOW was 25/29 correct (86.21% precision, 41.67% recall); HIGH was 27/28 correct (96.43% precision, 45% recall).

Chronological 30-swing blocks were 100%/50%, 86.67%/43.33%, 92.31%/40%, and 85.71%/40% for precision/recall respectively. Median entry was 1.21% away from the Local extreme and still retained about 44.8% of the full swing.

Important: the 40 bps and 0.45 thresholds were selected after studying this same 30-day archive. These numbers are therefore historical research evidence, not untouched OOS proof. The rule is now frozen and must earn promotion only through the independent forward first-signal gate.
