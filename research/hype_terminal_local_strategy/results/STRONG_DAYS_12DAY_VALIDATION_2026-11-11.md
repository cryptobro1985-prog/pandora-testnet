## What is actually universal in these 12 days

The transferable core is **not**:
- a fixed reversal percentage;
- a fixed OI sign;
- a fixed liquidation amount;
- a fixed book-imbalance threshold;
- a new composite score.

The repeated relationship is:

1. A same-side structural extreme appears.
2. The extreme stops extending.
3. Price begins responding in the opposite direction.
4. Opposite-side price impact becomes materially more efficient.
5. The old side can still show aggressive flow, but that flow stops creating continuation — the key absorption / efficiency-collapse effect.
6. When L50 is valid, replenishment / defense shifts in a way consistent with the transfer of control.
7. When liquidation data are valid, they are confirmation of exhaustion, not a mandatory zero-filled condition.
8. OI is interpreted through price + flow together:
   - reversal + falling OI can mean old-side unwind;
   - reversal + rising OI can mean new-side positioning.
   Therefore OI sign alone is not a Local rule.

## Trading interpretation

For the strategy, the earliest strong common observation point among the already agreed windows is **180 seconds**.

A trade should not be triggered because “price moved X%”.
The factual terminal-local condition is that the candidate survives and the market demonstrates **control transfer**:

- old side no longer extends the extreme;
- price moves away from it;
- new-side price impact strengthens;
- old-side flow efficiency collapses / absorption appears;
- rich layers confirm where available.

LOW is the long mirror.
HIGH is the short mirror.

The 60-second window can show the beginning of the transfer but separates true and false attempts less clearly.
By 300 seconds the same relationship remains visible, but part of the move has already been given away.
Therefore 180 seconds is the strongest observed compromise in these 12 strong days.
